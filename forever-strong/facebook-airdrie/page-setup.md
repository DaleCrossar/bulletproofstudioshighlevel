# Forever Strong Fitness Airdrie: Facebook page + Instagram setup

The messaging comes from Episode 1 and the brand kit:
- **Tagline:** *Building your physical pension.*
- **Who it's for:** people who don't think they're "gym people".
- **What it is:** small-group strength training, where each session you try to do a little better than last time.
- **Offer:** free trial sessions from December. The CTA is to join the waitlist.

Replace **[WAITLIST LINK]** with your waitlist page URL before saving.

```
Free trial sessions start in December. Join the waitlist to get first dibs on a spot.

  ```
