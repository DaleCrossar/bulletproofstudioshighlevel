# Powerful Hooks — 10/10 Viral Hook Types

Source: Instagram reel by Sam Gaudet (@sam.gaudet), "10/10 Viral Hooks"
(https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfxLbrVStRL/). Transcribed from the on-screen cards.

Each hook is a fill-in-the-blank template. Replace every `[bracket]` with something
specific to the client's niche, audience and offer. Don't leave the wording generic.

---

## 1. Contrarian Hooks
Use when: you're challenging common advice or a popular belief in the niche.

1. [Topic] isn't dead. It's just misunderstood.
2. [Common advice] is the worst advice in [niche] right now.
3. Never, ever, ever [common action].
4. You don't have a [problem] problem. You have a [real problem] problem.
5. [Popular thing] is telling you what you want to hear.
6. Doing more [action] won't fix your [problem].
7. [Popular method] is overrated. [Underrated method] is where the results are.
8. Everyone's chasing [goal]. That's exactly why you shouldn't.
9. [Belief] is a lie [niche] keeps telling itself.
10. Forget [common goal]. Aim for [better goal].

## 2. Curiosity Gap Hooks
Use when: you're holding back an answer so the viewer has to keep watching.

1. Don't say [common phrase]. Say [better phrase].
2. [Average people] [action]. [Top performers] [better action].
3. [Unlikely group] are better at [result] than [expected group].
4. There's a reason [successful people] never [common action].
5. The [thing] you ignore is why [problem] keeps happening.
6. Why do [beginners] sometimes beat [experts] at [skill]?
7. Every [top performer] in [niche] does this one thing first.
8. This is the [topic] iceberg. The real answers are at the bottom.
9. Let me tell you a secret nobody in [niche] talks about.
10. What's the [name] rule? It solves [problem].

## 3. Story Lead-In Hooks
Use when: the video is a personal story, lesson, mistake or result.

1. Not proud of it, but man enough to say it: [mistake].
2. I thought I'd be at [goal] by now.
3. I asked [person] what they'd do about [problem]. Their answer surprised me.
4. I almost quit [thing] right before it worked.
5. I did [action] every day for [time]. Here's what happened.
6. The worst [decision] I ever made taught me [lesson].
7. I got [result] without [thing everyone says you need].
8. Someone told me [criticism]. Here's what I did with it.
9. If I started [goal] from zero tomorrow, this is exactly what I'd do.
10. I spent [time] doing [method] wrong so you don't have to.

## 4. Signaling Hooks
Use when: you want to call out one specific audience so the right people stop scrolling.

1. If you know what [niche term] means, this one's for you.
2. You know that feeling when [frustrating moment]?
3. You're not [bad label]. You just don't know how to [action].
4. "[Common excuse]." No. [Real reason].
5. Every [role] has a [thing] they're scared to [action].
6. If [problem] makes your stomach drop, keep watching.
7. This is for the [audience] who's tired of [problem].
8. [Niche] people, you already know [shared pain].
9. If you've ever [specific mistake], this is the fix.
10. The moment your [thing] [fails] is exactly when you need this.

## 5. Labeling Hooks
Use when: you're using an "If you… then…" line so viewers put themselves in a group.

1. If you want [result], you're going to have to [contrarian action].
2. If you're not getting [result], you're probably missing this one thing.
3. If you [behavior], you're [label].
4. If you've tried [method] and it didn't work, check [overlooked step].
5. If you're still [habit], this is your sign to [action].
6. If you're [audience] and you want [result], listen closely.
7. If [problem] keeps happening, look at [overlooked cause].
8. If you need [thing] before you [action], you're [label].
9. If [warning sign] sounds familiar, you're [label].
10. If you can't explain [topic] simply, you don't understand it yet.

## 6. Framework Hooks
Use when: you're teaching a named rule, step-by-step method or checklist.

1. The [name] rule keeps your [team] from [problem].
2. [Number] things decide every [topic]: [list].
3. Run the [name] test before you [action].
4. The [number]-step [method] I use for [result].
5. Everything in [topic] comes down to [principle].
6. [Step one], [step two], [step three]. That's how you [action].
7. Good, better, best: [number] ways to [action].
8. The old way vs the new way to [action].
9. Run every [decision] through these [number] questions.
10. One rule for [topic]: [rule].

## 7. Authority Hooks
Use when: you're leaning on experience, results, research or well-known names.

1. I've helped [person] get [result]. Here's what actually worked.
2. After [time] in [niche], this is the one thing I'd keep.
3. I've studied [number] [examples]. The best ones share [pattern].
4. [Famous person] does [action] every [time]. Here's why.
5. [Expert] told me one thing that changed how I [action].
6. The best [role] in [niche] all [action]. Here's why.
7. [Company] spent [amount] learning this lesson.
8. I asked [expert] how they [action]. The answer: [surprise].
9. [Number] [people] in [niche] you should be studying.
10. Here's what [top performers] know about [topic] that you don't.

## 8. Stakes Hooks
Use when: you're showing the cost of getting it wrong or doing nothing.

1. You're one [mistake] away from losing [result].
2. Don't [big decision] until you can answer these questions.
3. Here's how to absolutely ruin your [thing].
4. Never [risky action]. Here's why.
5. [Thing] is changing. The [audience] who adapt win.
6. Every [time] you [delay], [consequence].
7. Skip [step] and [consequence].
8. The [thing] you never changed is costing you [result].
9. [Thing] is the one thing you can't get wrong in [niche].
10. Ignore [warning sign] and you'll pay for it in [cost].

## 9. Promise Stack Hooks
Use when: you're promising a clear result and naming the mechanism behind it.

1. There's a name for why [problem] happens. It's called [mechanism].
2. One change to your [thing] and [result]. I call it [mechanism].
3. [Result] comes down to a [number]-second rule. Here it is.
4. I call this [mechanism]. It's why [impressive result].
5. The fix for [problem] fits in one sentence. Then I'll show you how.
6. [Number] steps to [result]. The last one is where most people quit.
7. This one habit does the work of [number] others. [Mechanism].
8. You can [result] without [sacrifice]. It's called [mechanism].
9. By the end of this you'll know why [problem] happens and how to fix it.

## 10. Mind Reading Hooks
Use when: you're describing the viewer's situation so accurately they feel seen.

1. Let me guess. You [behavior], then [behavior], then wonder why [problem].
2. You've watched [number] videos on [topic] and still haven't [action].
3. You open [app] to [intention] and close it an hour later with nothing done.
4. You tell people you're [identity]. Your [evidence] says otherwise.
5. Right now you're [situation], and you already know it isn't working.
6. You know exactly what to do about [problem]. You just haven't done it.
7. You're not stuck on [topic]. You're scared of [real fear].
8. You've saved videos like this before. You never used them.
9. Your [thing] looks fine from the outside. Inside, [hidden problem].
