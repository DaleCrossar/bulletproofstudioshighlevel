# Social Scripts

Use this skill when writing short-form video scripts or social post openers.

## Knowledge files (Powerful Hooks)

- `references/powerful-hooks.md`: 10 hook types with 98 templates, and when to use each type.
  Source: Sam Gaudet (@sam.gaudet), "10/10 Viral Hooks" reel.

## How to use the hooks

1. **Choose the hook type that fits the video.** The "Use when" line under each type
   says what it's for. As a rough guide:
   - Personal story → Story Lead-In
   - Busting a myth → Contrarian
   - Teaching a method → Framework or Promise Stack
   - Reaching a specific audience → Signaling or Labeling
   - Showing what's at risk → Stakes
   - Building trust → Authority
   - Making the viewer feel seen → Mind Reading
2. **Fill every `[bracket]` with something specific to the client:** their niche, audience,
   real numbers, real results and local details. Generic fills give generic hooks.
3. **Write 3–5 hook options per script,** each from a different hook type, so the client can
   choose or A/B test them. Label each option with its type.
4. **Keep the hook to the first 1–3 seconds,** roughly 15 words or fewer when spoken. Put the
   key words in on-screen text as well.
5. **Make the script deliver what the hook promises.** A curiosity gap must be closed, a
   framework must be laid out step by step, and a story must reach its payoff.
6. **Never invent claims.** Authority, Stakes and Promise Stack hooks need real proof from the
   client, such as results, years in business or client numbers. If a fact isn't known, leave it
   as a bracket and flag it for the client to fill in.
