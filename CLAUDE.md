# Project notes for Claude

## Memory Room questions
When writing, reviewing, or suggesting questions for the **Memory Room**, first read
[`memory-room/question-craft.md`](memory-room/question-craft.md) and follow it. It was learned from a full study of the
@freeyourmiind_ TikTok account, which the owner considers the benchmark for powerful questions.

In short:
- Use the proven shapes: one-line verdict ("if you wrote a book about X, what would the last sentence be?"),
  the room ("who would you look for first?"), self across time with a constraint, meeting a parent before they were
  a parent, words never heard, who stays, and what you inherited.
- Name a specific person, place, or moment. Add a constraint. One sentence, 10–20 words. The answer should be a name or a sentence.
- Aim for an ache rather than an analysis. Prepare people before a deep question, give warmth after it, show one
  question per screen, and keep the audio wordless.
- Check every question against the checklist in section 6 of the guide.

The ranked source bank is [`memory-room/freeyourmind-question-bank.md`](memory-room/freeyourmind-question-bank.md).
